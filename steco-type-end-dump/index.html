The story behind the “STECO type”

For years, Gallegos manufactured end dump trailers for the STECO brand — and the trade adopted the name. Across the U.S. Northeast — New York, New Hampshire, Massachusetts, Rhode Island and the surrounding region — scrap and demolition haulers still ask for this unit as the “STECO type.” Today you can buy the same proven design directly from the manufacturer that built it.

The STECO type is a heavy-duty end dump engineered for high-abrasion loads: scrap metal, demolition debris and mixed C&D. It is available with a capacity of 80 to 96 cubic yards and lengths configurable from 38 to 42 feet, and it can be built in your choice of HARDOX® 450 or HARDOX® 500 Tuf wear steel.
